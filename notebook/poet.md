# "What Outshines You?"
```bash
Your gaze can the universe hold more grace?
Oceans bow, silenced by your calm.
Stars may glitter, yet vanish near your light.
Hearts still wonder,  what outshines you?
Even time yields, without reply.
```
# "Bulan Merah Jambu"
```bash
Yang luruh perlahan di pelupuk jingga
Ombak rindu yang bersauh di dermaga teduh
Selarik ikrar bersemi di angka dua puluh sembilan
Hangat dekapanmu melukis bulan merah jambu
Elokmu ialah akhir dari segala pengembaraan
```
